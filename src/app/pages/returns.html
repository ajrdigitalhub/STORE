Return Policy

1. Custom 3D Prints

Due to the personalized nature of 3D printed products, we generally do not accept returns for custom-made items unless they arrive damaged or defective. Each piece is manufactured specifically for your order.

2. Damaged or Defective Items

If your order arrives damaged or with a manufacturing defect, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the issue. We will evaluate the claim and, if approved, provide a replacement at no additional cost.

3. Standard Products

For non-customized, standard inventory items, you may request a return within 14 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer.

4. Refund Process

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, your refund will be processed, and a credit will automatically be applied to your original method of payment within 7-10 business days.

5. Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled within 2 hours of placement. Once the 3D printing process has started, we cannot accept cancellations.
